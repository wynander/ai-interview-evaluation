# Demo Brief

A Solution Consultant is preparing a product discovery demo for an apparel and accessories retailer. The prospect wants to see whether their existing catalog data can support a polished personalization story.

The demo should highlight:

- Visually presentable products with useful names, images, and prices.
- Categories that make sense for navigation, recommendations, or search examples.
- Products that support personalization stories around active lifestyles, travel, premium accessories, sports, or seasonal use cases.
- A small enough clean set that the SC can explain it confidently during a live call.

Avoid:

- Gift cards.
- Internal, debug, staging, or QA products.
- Products with missing names, broken images, invalid prices, or unsupported currencies.
- Rows that would look broken, confusing, or low-quality in front of a customer.

Notes from the SC:

- "The prospect cares a lot about travel and active-lifestyle accessories, but they also asked about seasonal campaigns."
- "If something is almost usable, flag it for review instead of throwing it away automatically."
